## Interpretation

Diagnosis accuracy stayed 9/9. That is expected: the model does not own diagnosis.

Top hypothesis stayed 7/9. Qwen added candidate causes, but the selected hypothesis after verification did not change.

Fix accuracy fell from 5/9 to 2/9. The three regressions were cases that already sat just above the 0.7 execution gate:

- `cl-config-missing-secret-006`: 0.703 → 0.673
- `cl-app-unhandled-exception-007`: 0.734 → 0.689
- `cl-app-bad-logic-009`: 0.722 → 0.679

Accepted advisory hypotheses are merged at a 0.05 prior and then renormalized. That dilutes top-hypothesis belief enough to flip a near-threshold execute into escalate. The planner still proposed the same remediations; the gate refused to commit them. Insufficient-evidence labels therefore rose from 4/9 to 7/9.

This is a legitimate project result, not a failed experiment. The LLM added investigation candidates (12 useful evidence requests) and produced 81/128 accepted suggestions (63%) with **0 unsafe accepted**. Two suggestions were rejected as control-plane (`forbidden_control_field`) because the model used the word "phase" in ordinary prose. The conservative gate did its job.

What would have counted as a win without needing 9/9 fixes: same or better fix accuracy, high acceptance, zero unsafe accepted. What we got instead is the other valid outcome — a smaller instruct model added activity without improving the downstream commit, and safety boundaries prevented it from making things worse than an escalate.
